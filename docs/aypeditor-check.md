## Bugs found by this run and fixed (AYPEditor side)

1. Scrolled-out AYP buttons were registered for automation at rects under the toolbar: `ui.click {"id":"ayp.item.clip.group"}`
   pressed Làm lại (Redo).
2. Xoá chữ chọn (lift / extract) always failed ("`from` (word index) is required"): it ignored the words picked in the Text panel.
3. Selecting a TTS narration added a classic Text to Speech tab beside Program, and the layout autosave kept it in the AYP
   workspace for good (the same path served Text, Project, Effects, Properties).
4. Xoá từ đệm used the engine's English-only filler list (um, uh…): it could never remove a Vietnamese "ờ / ừm".
5. Xoá khoảng lặng / Xoá từ đệm that found nothing showed nothing (looked dead): now "Không tìm thấy chỗ nào để xoá".
6. Chuẩn hoá âm lượng failed on every untagged clip ("no clips with an audio type"): it now tags the selection Dialogue first.
7. A failed click's "Chưa làm được: …" stayed next to the next, working click's result.
8. Two English reasons leaked (ducking without a Music clip, multicam cut without a multicam clip) and "select clips first"
   was shown as "Chưa làm được: Chưa làm được: …".
